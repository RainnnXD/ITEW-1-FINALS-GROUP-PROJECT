What would you love to do?

Whether you want to relax, get outdoors, or learn something new, there is a trip for it.

Coast & Islands

Beach Days and Island Hopping

Swim, snorkel, hop between islands, or just spend a slow day by the sea.

Try: Palawan, Boracay, Bohol, Siargao

Nature

Hikes, Hills, and Waterfalls

Mountain views, scenic trails, and cool waterfalls to rinse off the heat.

Try: Banaue, Baguio, Camiguin, Hulugan Falls

History & Heritage

Walk Through Historic Places

Heritage streets, old churches, museums, and landmarks full of stories.

Try: Intramuros, Vigan, Cebu City, Iloilo

Before you go

  • Check the weather and local conditions.
  • Respect local customs and community rules.
  • Bring water, sun protection, and a reusable bottle.
  • Keep natural places clean and follow safety advice.
