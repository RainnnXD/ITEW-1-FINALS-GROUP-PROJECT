A Weekend in Baguio

October 4, 2026

The morning fog rolled through the mountains as we started our walk around the city. The air was cool and carried the scent of pine trees — a reminder that we were somewhere truly different from the lowlands. Baguio greeted us with cold air, pine forests, and a pace of life that felt worlds away from the city below. By evening, strawberry taho and warm café lights made the highland city feel like home.
