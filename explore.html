Explore the Philippines

An overview of Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

Luzon Visayas Mindanao At a glance
NORTH
Northern island group

Luzon

The largest island group, home to the capital, Manila. Go for mountain towns, rice terraces, heritage streets, and surf coasts.

  • Known for: Banaue rice terraces, Vigan, Taal Volcano, Mayon Volcano
  • Main gateways: Manila and Clark
  • Best time: November to April
BanaueViganLa UnionSagadaBaler
Explore Luzon
CENTRAL
Central island group

Visayas

Hundreds of islands made for hopping. Expect white-sand beaches, whale sharks, old churches, and lively festivals.

  • Known for: Boracay, Bohol Chocolate Hills, Oslob whale sharks, Sinulog
  • Main gateways: Cebu, Iloilo, Caticlan, and Tagbilaran
  • Best time: November to May
BoracayBoholCebuSiquijorMalapascua
Explore Visayas
SOUTH
Southern island group

Mindanao

The second-largest island group, with surf breaks, crater lakes, the country's highest peak, and some of its richest cultures.

  • Known for: Siargao surfing, Camiguin, Mount Apo, Kadayawan Festival
  • Main gateways: Davao, Cagayan de Oro, and Siargao
  • Best time: Dry for much of the year. Siargao surf peaks September to November.
SiargaoCamiguinDavaoLake SebuMount Apo
