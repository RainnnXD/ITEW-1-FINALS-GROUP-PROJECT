Places to Fall in Love With

Discover the landscapes, culture, and attractions of Mindanao.

Highland Escape

Bukidnon

Enjoy wide-open landscapes, cool highlands, and views of rolling green hills.

Island Adventure

Siargao

Explore palm-lined roads, lagoons, and the island's well-known surfing scene.

Nature & Waterfalls

Iligan

Visit scenic waterfalls and enjoy the lush natural surroundings of the City of Majestic Waterfalls.

Mountain Views

Davao

Explore local markets, enjoy regional food, and discover nature destinations near the city.

Coastal Break

Camiguin

Discover volcanic landscapes, hot and cold springs, and beautiful coastal views.

Culture & Cuisine

Zamboanga

Experience local culture and taste dishes influenced by the region's many traditions.

Before You Go

Travel Notes

01 · Learn Local Customs

Be respectful of local communities, traditions, and cultural sites.

02 · Plan Transport Ahead

Travel times can be long between destinations, so check routes and transport schedules in advance.

03 · Travel Prepared

Check weather and local travel advisories, and bring suitable gear for your planned activities.
