Explore the Philippines · Island Adventures

Discover Visayas

Blue waters, island culture & unforgettable coastlines

The Visayas invites you to slow down, explore island communities, discover local traditions, and enjoy clear waters and scenic shores.

Explore Destinations

Must-Visit Places

Discover the islands, culture, and attractions of Visayas.

Island Getaway

Boracay

Relax on White Beach, enjoy island activities, and catch a colorful sunset by the sea.

Natural Wonder

Bohol

Visit the Chocolate Hills, explore the countryside, and learn about the island's wildlife and natural attractions.

City & Culture

Cebu

Explore historic landmarks, try Cebu lechon, and visit nearby waterfalls and beaches.

Coastal Escape

Bantayan Island

Enjoy peaceful beaches, laid-back towns, and fresh seafood beside the sea.

Marine Life

Moalboal

Discover marine life and popular snorkeling and diving areas along Cebu's coastline.

Heritage & Food

Iloilo

Explore heritage architecture and taste local specialties such as La Paz batchoy.

Travel Notes

01 · Respect the Sea

Follow local guidance and avoid touching coral or disturbing marine life.

02 · Explore Local Food

Try regional dishes and support small local eateries, markets, and food businesses.

03 · Check Island Transfers

Confirm ferry schedules and weather advisories before traveling between islands.

The Island Paradise

Discover the Beauty of Visayas

The Visayas is a breathtaking region known for its crystal-clear waters, pristine white-sand beaches, vibrant festivals, and charming island communities. Whether you seek tropical adventures, cultural discoveries, marine wonders, or authentic local cuisine, the Visayas offers unforgettable experiences in every island.
