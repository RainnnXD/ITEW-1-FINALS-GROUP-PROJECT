Discover Luzon

Mountain mornings, heritage towns & tropical shores

From cool mountain air and centuries-old streets to volcanic landscapes and beautiful coastlines, Luzon offers countless ways to explore the Philippines.

Places to Fall in Love With

Discover the landscapes, culture, and attractions of Luzon.

Mountain Escape

Baguio

Enjoy cool weather, pine trees, beautiful gardens, and lively local markets in the Summer Capital of the Philippines.

Living Heritage

Banaue

Admire the famous rice terraces and learn about the traditions and heritage of local communities in the Cordillera region.

Heritage Town

Vigan

Walk along historic streets, admire preserved Spanish-colonial architecture, and experience Ilocano culture and cuisine.

Coastal Days

Batangas

Discover beaches, quiet coves, and snorkeling spots along the coast. It is a great option for a relaxing seaside trip.

Volcano Views

Bicol

See the iconic Mayon Volcano, discover scenic landscapes, and sample flavorful Bicolano dishes such as Bicol Express.

Island Paradise

Palawan

Explore turquoise lagoons, limestone cliffs, and beautiful island scenery in one of the country's well-known travel destinations.

Before You Go

Travel Notes

01 · Start Early

Visit popular attractions in the morning for a more relaxed experience and more time to explore.

02 · Taste the Region

Try local favorites such as Vigan empanada, Bicol Express, and other regional specialties.

03 · Pack for the Weather

Bring layers for cooler highlands and light, comfortable clothing for warm coastal areas.
