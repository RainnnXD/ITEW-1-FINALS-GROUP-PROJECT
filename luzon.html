Discover Luzon

Mountain mornings, heritage towns & tropical shores

From breathtaking mountain landscapes and refreshing waterfalls to historic cities and beautiful beaches, discover the wonders of Luzon, the largest island group in the Philippines.

Explore Destinations

Must-Visit Places

Explore some of the most beautiful and memorable places across Luzon.

Mountain Escape

Baguio

Enjoy cool weather, pin -covered landscapes, local markets, and relaxing views in the Summer Capital of the Philippines.

Green Landscape

Banaue

Admire the famous rice terraces and learn about the traditions and heritage of local communities in the Cordillera region.

Heritage Town

Vigan

Walk along historic streets, admire preserved Spanish-colonial architecture, and experience Ilocano culture and cuisine.

Coastal Days

Batangas

Discover beaches, quiet coves, and snorkeling spots along the coast. It is a great option for a relaxing seaside trip.

Volcano Views

Bicol

See the iconic Mayon Volcano, discover scenic landscapes, and sample flavorful Bicolano dishes such as Bicol Express.

Island Paradise

Palawan

Explore turquoise lagoons, limestone cliffs, and beautiful island scenery in one of the country's well-known travel destinations.

Travel Tips for Luzon

A few simple tips to help you enjoy your Luzon adventure.

01 / WEATHER

Check the Forecast

Check local weather advisories before visiting mountains, islands, and coastal destinations.

02 / ESSENTIALS

Pack Smart

Bring comfortable footwear, sunscreen, drinking water, and appropriate clothing for your destination.

03 / CULTURE

Respect Local Traditions

Respect local customs, follow site rules, and support local businesses and communities.

04 / ENVIRONMENT

Travel Responsibly

Keep destinations clean, minimize waste, and protect the natural environment.
